# Photograph provenance

Both photographs are **client-supplied**: they come from the old Quantum-BioTesting repository (`assets/images/sample-collection.webp` and `report-review.webp`). That repository holds no source, photographer or licence record for them, so their rights are unconfirmed. Treat them as provisional until the client confirms the rights or replaces them with commissioned photography.

| File | Subject | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `client-clinician-and-patient.webp` | Clinician applying a plaster to a patient's arm after a sample (the approved hero photograph) | 1200×801 |
| `client-clinician-explaining-results.webp` | Clinician explaining results to a colleague at a computer | 900×600 |

Neither depicts identifiable Quantum staff or customers as far as is known; the page captions them "Illustrative photograph".

An earlier pass used CC0 lifestyle photography (coffee, flowers, beach walk, a cut orange, a mother and child). It was removed because the images did not share a visual style. They remain in git history only.

**Processing** (`scripts/make-photo-assets.mjs`): one shared grade applied to both, so they read as a set: shadows pulled toward the logo navy, highlights toward warm cream, colour muted by about 10%, a gentle contrast curve. Colour grading only; no retouching.
