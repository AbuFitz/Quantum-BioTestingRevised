# Design research: direction v3 ("a good local")

**Brief.** Quantum should feel welcoming, modern and human: closer to a well-run independent café, studio or neighbourhood shop than a laboratory. The service is private blood testing, but that does not need to set every visual choice.

## References and what was taken from each

| Reference | Observed | Used (as a quality, not a copy) | Not used |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BioClin** (client's reference) | Literal navigation, a single "Book Now" action, photography-led presentation, readable package comparisons, consistent icons | Short literal nav and one enquiry action; two offers that compare easily; one icon family | Pinned process sequence; full-height sections; its identity |
| **Thriva** (UK blood testing; Plus Jakarta Sans; green `#2AC669`, apricot `#F7B657`, coral `#FC687E`, plum) | A health brand proving that a bright, friendly palette and rounded geometry are credible in this category | Permission for a fresh, warm palette; colour-coding offers by colour | Its palette and typeface |
| **Dishoom** (hospitality; cream `#F0ECE0`, terracotta `#E1A894`, deep green) | Warm paper canvas, earthy accents, confident serif/sans pairings, imagery of people and place | Cream canvas; one deep brand-colour chapter on a warm page; people and place in the photography | Its illustration, palette, wording |
| **GAIL's** (bakery; cream `#F5F5ED`, ink) | Quiet cream backgrounds, big friendly type, generous colour fields, no ornament | Large calm colour fields, one strong headline voice | |
| **Medichecks** (UK blood testing; deep navy `#01183A`) | The category's default is dark navy and clinical neon; Quantum's logo blue could slip into this | A reason to keep navy to a single chapter and let warm colour lead | Dark-and-neon treatment |

## Typefaces tested on the real copy at phone width

Headline "Private blood tests, without the fuss.", "Men’s Health Check", "£995 RRP £2,112 · 300+ · Up to 350", and the mobile menu words, set on cream in the logo blue: Bricolage Grotesque, Fraunces (soft axes), Gabarito, Familjen Grotesk, Outfit, Young Serif; body candidates Instrument Sans, DM Sans, Onest. Chosen: **Bricolage Grotesque** for headlines, prices and menu (confident, characterful, legible at 40px, sturdy numerals) and **DM Sans** for text and controls (geometric, echoing the logotype). Fraunces rendered as a plain light serif and Young Serif's oldstyle figures made prices wobble. Rejected: Newsreader and Figtree (previous pass), the usual healthcare sans families.

## Palette (roles)
Cream `#fcf6ec` canvas · sand `#f3e6cf` surface · **blue `#013275` (logo): headlines, buttons and the one full colour chapter** · sage `#d2e3ce` (Men's) · apricot `#f9d4b2` (Women's) · butter `#f8e4a6` (closing) · coral `#e4573d` (the Q's red, warmed) for accents, `#b93d26` for small coral text. Both choice colours are shown in the hero's two circles so the page introduces its own system.

## Shapes
Arches and circles (the Q's roundness, and the familiar shape of a window or doorway), soft 28px panels, pill buttons. No hairline-and-grid "report" look.
